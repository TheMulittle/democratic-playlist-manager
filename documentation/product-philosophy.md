# Democractic Playlist Manager

The Democractic Playlist Manager intends to solve a major problem when it comes to shared playlists: how to ensure people listening to the same playlist (such in a party or in a live stream) have the opportunity to add their own songs without, for example, waiting from many songs from a single person that happened to start adding songs earlier

That was the foundation that created this project, the intention is to extend it for other features that increase the quality of life and makes more interesting playing songs toghether
